# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a *science fiction horror* series set in the 1980s in the town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of kids who face supernatural events and secret experiments.

> "Friends don't lie." - Eleven

## Main Characters
- Eleven
- Mike Wheeler
- Dustin Henderson
- Lucas Sinclair
- Will Byers

## Why I Like It
1. Exciting and suspenseful storyline
2. Great characters and friendships
3. Amazing 1980s music and setting
